# Mount Isa Economic Observatory - Community Showcase

## 🎯 What We've Built

A **transparent, AI-powered system** that tracks every dollar flowing into North West Queensland communities - showing where money comes from, where it goes, and where opportunities exist for local economic development.

## 💡 What This Tells Us (Applying Jane Jacobs' Import Replacement Theory)

### Current State: "Leaky Bucket" Economy

```
Money IN (Government funding, mining wages)
    ↓
    95% LEAKS OUT (spent in Brisbane, Townsville, overseas)
    ↓
Money OUT (goods, services purchased externally)
```

### Opportunity: 10% Localization Target

**If we reduced leakage by just 10 percentage points:**
- More local jobs
- Stronger local businesses
- Multiplier effect: Every $1 spent locally circulates 3-4 times
- Economic resilience when mining cycles change

## 📚 Economics Theory We're Testing

### Jane Jacobs - Import Replacement
> "Cities grow by replacing imports with local production"

**Mount Isa Test:**
- Can we identify $10M of currently imported services?
- Can we build capacity to deliver 10% locally?
- Does this create the "multiplier effect" Jacobs predicted?

### Preston Model - Community Wealth Building
> "Anchor institutions can drive local economy through procurement"

**Mount Isa Anchors:**
- Mount Isa City Council
- Mount Isa Hospital
- Glencore mining operations
- TAFE North West Queensland

**Question:** If these anchors committed to 10% local procurement increase, what happens?

### New Economics Foundation - LM3 (Local Multiplier 3)
> "Track how far money circulates in local economy"

**Measurement:**
- Round 1: Initial spend
- Round 2: Where recipients spend it
- Round 3: Where those recipients spend it
- **LM3 score:** Total local circulation
