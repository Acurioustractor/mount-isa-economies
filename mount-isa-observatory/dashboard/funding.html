Key Insight: Mount Isa Leads

Mount Isa has secured $53.38M - the highest absolute funding of any Queensland community for youth justice programs. This represents:

  • $2,885 per person - 64× higher than Gold Coast ($45/person)
  • 26% of total tracked funding across all 9 communities
  • 7 diverse programs including $24M On-Country and $20M Co-Responder
  • Potential $26.75M annual economic impact if funds circulate locally (LM3 multiplier)

Per Capita Leaders

On a per-person basis, Mount Isa ranks #2 with $2,885 per capita:

Remote and regional communities receive higher per-capita funding to account for service delivery costs and infrastructure gaps.
