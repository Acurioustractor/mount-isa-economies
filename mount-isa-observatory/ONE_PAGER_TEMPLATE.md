## 🔍 How We Track It

### The System

**Stage 1: Announcements** (Media Statements)
- Source: Queensland Government media releases
- Confidence: Medium (what was promised)

**Stage 2: Budget** (Budget Papers)
- Source: Queensland Budget, Service Delivery Statements
- Confidence: High (what was allocated)

**Stage 3: Payments** (ACNC, Contracts)
- Source: ACNC financial reports, QLD Contract Directory
- Confidence: Highest (what was actually paid)

**Stage 4: Outcomes** (Evaluation Reports)
- Source: Annual reports, academic studies, audits
- Confidence: Varies (what was achieved)

### Data Quality

Every number links to its source:
- 📄 **Document**: Media statement, budget paper, financial report
- 📅 **Date**: When it was announced/budgeted/paid
- ⭐ **Confidence**: 1-5 scale (1=unverified, 5=payment confirmed)
- ✅ **Status**: Announced, Budgeted, Paid, or Verified
