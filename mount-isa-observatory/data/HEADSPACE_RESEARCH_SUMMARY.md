## FUNDING BREAKDOWN

### **Commonwealth Funding**
- **Core PHN funding:** $50-100M+ annually (estimated)
- **Recent major announcements:**
  - $320M+ national expansion to 173 services by 2025-26
  - $23.5M for 3 QLD centers (Bribie, Caloundra, North Brisbane) 2023
  - $44M to 57 centers nationally for demand management
  - $27M for wait time reduction
  - $29.7M for Early Career Program

### **Queensland State Funding**
- **Better Care Together Plan:** $1.645B total mental health spending
- **Headspace enhancement:** $75.3M to enhance headspace centers
- **In-Reach Program:** Metro South Health staff at 4 locations (Capalaba, Meadowbrook, Beaudesert, Inala)

### **Typical Center Funding**
- Small/satellite: $1.5-2.5M/year
- Standard centers: $2-4M/year
- Large metro centers: $3-5M/year
- Specific known: Strathpine $3.5M (establishment), grants up to $1.8M

## SERVICES PROVIDED

All headspace centers provide free support for young people aged 12-25 including:
- Mental health support
- Alcohol and other drug (AOD) services
- General practitioner (GP) services
- Vocational and educational support
- Peer support programs
- Family support
- Online counseling (eheadspace)

**Model:** Co-located multidisciplinary teams in youth-friendly spaces
