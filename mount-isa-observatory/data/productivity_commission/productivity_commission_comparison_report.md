# Productivity Commission Youth Justice Data

**Source**: Report on Government Services 2024, Chapter 17: Youth Justice Services
**Data Period**: 2022-23 financial year
**Published**: January 2024

---

## 💰 Cost Comparison

### Average cost per day in detention

- **Queensland**: $1305.00/dollars
- **National**: $1421.00/dollars

### Average cost per day for community supervision

- **Queensland**: $97.00/dollars
- **National**: $84.00/dollars

## 📊 Outcomes

### Youth justice recidivism rate (12 months)

- **Queensland**: 52.3%
- **National**: 49.7%
- **Queensland (Indigenous)**: 57.8%
- **National (Indigenous)**: 55.2%

## 👥 Population in System

### Average daily number of young people in detention

- **Queensland**: 164 youth
- **National**: 740 youth

### Proportion of youth in detention who are Indigenous

- **Queensland**: 70.7%
- **National**: 59.3%

## 💡 Key Insights for Mount Isa

### Cost Savings from Community Programs

**Every youth diverted from detention to community supervision saves:**
- $1208.00/day
- $440,920/year

**If Mount Isa On-Country program diverts 10 youth from detention:**
- Annual savings: $4,409,200

### Indigenous Focus Needed

- 70.7% of QLD youth in detention are Indigenous
- Indigenous recidivism rate: 57.8% (higher than all-youth 52.3%)
- **On-Country programs like Mount Isa are culturally appropriate responses**
