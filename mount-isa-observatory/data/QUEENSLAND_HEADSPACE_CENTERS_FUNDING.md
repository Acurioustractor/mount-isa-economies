## FUNDING ANALYSIS

### **Federal Government Funding Programs**

#### 2019-20 Budget
- **$111.3 million** to increase headspace sites from 115 to 145 nationally
- **$95.7 million** annual funding for headspace network

#### 2021-22 Budget
- **$278.6 million** for 10 new sites and satellite upgrades
- **$765.8 million** total directed to headspace and Head to Health centers

#### 2022 - Demand Management and Enhancement Program
- **$44 million** total to 57 headspace centers nationally
  - $27 million for wait time reduction (31 centers)
  - $17 million for buildings and equipment (38 centers)
- **Queensland recipients:** Gladstone, Inala, Ipswich, Mackay, Maroochydore, Meadowbrook (including Beaudesert satellite), Nundah, Redcliffe, Strathpine, Toowoomba, Taringa, Woolloongabba

#### 2023 Announcements
- **$23.5 million** for new Queensland sites: Bribie Island, Caloundra, North Brisbane
- **Up to $1.8 million** grants per site for 40 headspace services across multiple states

#### 2024-25 Budget & Recent Announcements
- **$24.2 million** to reduce wait times at headspace services
- **$29.7 million** for headspace Early Career Program
- **$27.1 million** for Early Career Program expansion to early 2027
- **$320+ million** Albanese Government investment to expand headspace network to 173 services by 2025-26
- **NEW:** First Nations focused headspace Deception Bay announced (2025)

### **State Government Funding**

#### Queensland Better Care Together Plan
- **$1.645 billion** for mental health, AOD services across Queensland
- **$75.3 million** to enhance headspace centers (increase access to multidisciplinary services)
- Funding for 2 new headspace sites
- Funding for clinical in-reach into new and existing headspace sites
- **headspace In-Reach Program:** Metro South Health staff provide consultation, assessment, and brief intervention at 4 headspace locations: Capalaba, Meadowbrook, Beaudesert, and Inala
- Partnership model strengthens integration between state health services and Commonwealth-funded headspace
