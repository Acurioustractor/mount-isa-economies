## FUNDING STRUCTURE NOTES

### **How headspace is Funded**

Based on multiple sources, the funding structure is:

1. **Primary Funding:** Australian Government Department of Health and Aged Care
2. **Administration:** Via local Primary Health Networks (PHNs)
3. **Model:** PHNs commission local organizations to operate headspace centers
4. **Supplementary:**
   - Medicare billings (GPs and allied health professionals)
   - State government in-reach programs (e.g., Queensland's $75.3M)
   - Grants for specific enhancements (wait times, buildings, equipment)
   - Philanthropic donations (e.g., Gandel Foundation $1.2M)
   - Local government small grants

### **Typical Funding Components**

- **Core Commonwealth funding** covers: rent, infrastructure, some salaried staff
- **Medicare billings** fund: GP consultations, allied health services
- **Enhancement grants** fund: building upgrades, equipment, wait time reduction programs
- **State funding** covers: clinical in-reach, integration with state health services
- **Philanthropic/local** funds: Specific programs, school engagement, community outreach
