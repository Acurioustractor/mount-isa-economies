# Queensland Community Safety & Police Youth Programs - Comprehensive Research

**Research Date**: 2025-11-17
**Data Sources**: Queensland Government Ministerial Media Statements, Police News, Council Statements
**Method**: Comprehensive WebSearch across statements.qld.gov.au and related sources
**Coverage**: Statewide programs + 8 priority communities

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Total Identified Funding**: **$100M+ in community safety and police youth programs**

This research identified extensive community safety and police youth funding across Queensland, including:
- **$1.28 billion** Community Safety Plan (statewide)
- **$56 million** PCYC infrastructure and programs (statewide)
- **$115 million** Gold Standard Early Intervention programs
- **$50 million** Regional Reset program (9 locations)
- **$40 million** Youth Justice Schools (Logan & Cairns)
- **$24 million** Intensive On-Country programs
- **$5 million** Justice Reinvestment (First Nations)

Beyond traditional policing, Queensland has invested heavily in:
- PCYC facilities and programs
- Youth co-responder teams (13+ regions)
- Community safety plans
- Night patrols
- Crime prevention grants
- Early intervention initiatives

## MAJOR STATEWIDE PROGRAMS

### 1. Community Safety Plan for Queensland

**Total Investment**: $1.28 billion (over forward estimates)
**Announced**: April 2024 (Statement 100206)
**Department**: Queensland Police Service, Youth Justice, Premier
**URL**: https://statements.qld.gov.au/statements/100206

**Key Components**:
- 900 additional police personnel (including 500 police officers)
- 3,000 metal detecting wands
- 1,000 tasers
- POLAIR helicopters for Cairns, Far North QLD, Wide Bay, Sunshine Coast
- Extended Youth Co-Responder Teams
- Electronic monitoring expansion ($7 million)
- Upgraded police facilities (Hervey Bay, Bundaberg, Townsville, Mount Isa, others)

**Five Pillars**:
1. Supporting victims
2. Delivering for our frontline
3. Detaining offenders to protect the community
4. Intervening when people offend
5. Preventing crime before it occurs

**Beneficiary Communities**: Statewide (all Queensland communities)

### 5. Youth Co-Responder Teams

**Total Investment**: $100+ million (initial + extensions)
**Announced**: June 2023 & April 2024 (Statements 98003, 100172)
**Department**: Queensland Police Service & Youth Justice

**Funding Details**:
- Initial investment: $100 million (5 teams: Mount Isa, Toowoomba, Fraser Coast, South Brisbane, Ipswich)
- Extension funding: $13.55 million (Gold Coast, South Brisbane, Cairns, Sunshine Coast, South-West)
- Capacity increase: $11.2 million (July 2024, Statement 100864)

**Outcomes Reported**:
- 73% reduction in serious repeat offender reoffending
- Average offences dropped from 25 to 7 (6 months post-engagement)
- 14% reduction in serious repeat offenders who re-offend (since Oct 2023)

**Locations** (13+ regions):
- Mount Isa
- Townsville
- Cairns
- Mackay (5,000+ engagements in 3 years)
- Fraser Coast
- Toowoomba
- Logan (4,200 engagements since May 2020)
- Ipswich
- Gold Coast
- South Brisbane
- Sunshine Coast
- South-West regions
- Moreton

**URL**: https://statements.qld.gov.au/statements/100864

## CONCLUSION

This research has identified over **$100 million in community safety and police youth programs** across Queensland, with the total investment reaching **$1.6+ billion** when including the comprehensive Community Safety Plan and related initiatives.

Every major Queensland community has dedicated programs, from PCYC facilities to youth co-responder teams to cultural On-Country programs. The evidence shows these programs are working, with dramatic reductions in reoffending rates.

**This represents a fundamental shift in Queensland's approach to community safety** - from reactive policing to proactive prevention, from punishment to support, and from government-only solutions to community partnerships.
