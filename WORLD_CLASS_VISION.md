### 2. JUSTICE LENS: Who Benefits? Who's Excluded?

**Standard economic analysis asks:**
- "Is the economy growing?"
- "Are we efficient?"
- "What's the GDP?"

**Justice-centered analysis asks:**
- "Who controls the wealth?"
- "Are First Nations people benefiting from their land?"
- "Are the most vulnerable getting what they need?"
- "Is power being redistributed?"

#### Justice Metrics to Track

**Indigenous Economic Sovereignty**
- % of contracts to Indigenous businesses
- Indigenous business ownership rate vs population
- Indigenous employment in high-wage jobs
- Land use payments to Traditional Owners
- Cultural economy value (art, knowledge, tourism)
- Self-determination index (community control)

**Equity Measures**
- Gini coefficient (income inequality)
- Service access by postcode (rich vs poor areas)
- Disability service gaps
- Youth unemployment by background
- Single parent support adequacy
- Elder care quality (Indigenous vs non-Indigenous)

**Vulnerability & Safety**
- Housing affordability vs wages
- Food security (distance to affordable food)
- Domestic violence service capacity
- Mental health wait times
- Youth crisis support availability
- Legal aid accessibility

**Community Power**
- Local ownership % (businesses owned by locals)
- Worker cooperative count
- Community land trust holdings
- Participatory budgeting adoption
- Indigenous-controlled investment funds
- Community decision-making bodies

## The Ultimate Data Architecture

**Entities** (Nodes)

- 2,000+ local businesses (ABN registry)
- 500+ employers
- 50+ government agencies
- 100+ community organizations

- Traditional Owner groups

**Relationships** (Edges)

- Employer → Employee (wages)
- Government → Contractor (procurement)
- Service → Client (service delivery)
- Charity → Beneficiary (assistance)
- Business → Owner (ownership)
- Entity → Land (Traditional Owner connection)

**Flows** (Weighted Edges)
- Dollar amounts
- Service counts
- Time (hours, months)
- Social value (wellbeing impact)

- "Find all youth services with <3 month wait times"

   - Output: Predicted jobs, wellbeing, sovereignty gain
   - Use: Rank investment opportunities

3. **Equity Monitor**
   - Input: Service access, income, demographics
   - Output: Disparity alerts, gap identification
   - Use: Ensure vulnerable populations aren't left behind

4. **Community Voice Analyzer** (Already in your service map!)
   - Input: 127+ interviews, ongoing feedback
   - Output: Sentiment, needs, priorities
   - Use: Ground all decisions in community wisdom

**Justice Algorithms**

- **Kalkadoon Priority Score**: Weight investments by benefit to Traditional Owners
- **Vulnerability Index**: Prioritize those most in need
- **Self-Determination Multiplier**: Value community control over external "solutions"
